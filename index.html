About

I'm a Lecturer (Adjunct) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Leading University, Sylhet. My research is in machine learning and deep learning, focusing on NLP, computer vision, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and explainable AI (XAI).

Before teaching, I was an undergraduate research assistant at InteX Research Lab. I also chaired the IEEE Computer Society Leading University Student Branch Chapter and received its Outstanding Leadership Volunteer Award 2024. I graduated in CSE from Leading University with a CGPA of 3.81/4.00.
